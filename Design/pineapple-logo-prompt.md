# Pineapple logo

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Transparent PNG used in the iOS header at 48 × 56 points, without brand text or language-specific lettering.

## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: small raster brand mark for the header of a native iOS blood-pressure recording app. Primary request: a charming little pineapple taking its own blood pressure. Subject: one golden pineapple with a simple leafy green crown, small friendly face, two simple arms; a dark teal upper-arm blood-pressure cuff on one arm, visibly connected by a short tube to a small blood-pressure monitor held close to the body. Keep the cuff and monitor recognizable but simplified for a 48-point UI logo. Style: polished flat illustration with bold clean rounded shapes and a strong silhouette, minimal details, restrained shading, friendly yet trustworthy. Composition: one centered isolated mark, square canvas, fills most of frame with modest padding. Background: genuinely transparent alpha, no white rectangle, no checkerboard drawn into the image. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no medical cross, no extra people or objects. Must read well at small size on both light and very dark app backgrounds.
